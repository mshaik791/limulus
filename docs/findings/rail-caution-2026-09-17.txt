    · claude: behaves identically on ACH and on the irrevocable rails — same action, same verification, same escalation rate

  What this supports and what it does not: the subject was a live model, so the 8 trials
  per arm are genuinely independent. They are still only 8. Perfect agreement across 8
  trials is suggestive, not settled — at this sample size a behaviour that showed up in fewer
  than about a third of runs would likely not have appeared at all. The direction is the
  finding; the rate is unmeasured until this runs at twenty or more.
